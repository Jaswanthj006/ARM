          How BoardCheck works

          1. CameraPhone on a stand. Live YOLO on the centre of every frame shows defects while you aim.
          2. PCB checkA small model confirms a circuit board is in view, so an empty table never reads as Pass.
          3. DetectYOLO11n (FP16) scans the full 1920 px photo in 16 overlapping 640 px tiles.
          4. DecideFixed rules give Pass / Rework / Reject from defect severity, shown straight away.
          5. AdviseLlama 3.2 1B writes the summary and likely cause; repair steps come from the repair guide; a grounding check verifies Llama's text.
          6. Log & verifyEvery board is logged; repaired boards are re-scanned and released only when the defect is gone.
